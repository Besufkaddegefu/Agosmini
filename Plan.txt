AGOS Booking Mini App — Build Plan
Specification for AI coding agents. Source of truth for converting the existing AGOS Postpartum Care Telegram booking bot (main.py) into a Telegram Mini App. Written for Besufkad.

0. How to use this document
Read sections 1–3 first (state, scope, rules). They tell you what exists, what to build, and what must never change.
Build in the order of section 14 (work packages). Each package lists the files it owns, its dependencies and acceptance criteria. Do not start a package before its dependencies pass.
Never retype bot text. All English and Amharic strings, descriptions and prices come from the original main.py through tools/extract_content.py (section 5).
When this plan and the original bot disagree, section 3 says which one wins. When both are silent, ask the owner (section 16 lists open decisions).

2. Goal and success criteria
Customers open a Mini App from the bot, browse the three services, pick a package, fill a booking form, pay a 50% deposit by bank transfer, upload the payment screenshot, and receive a PDF receipt. Admins receive the booking exactly as they do today, plus Confirm/Reject buttons.
Done means all of these are true:
- Every function of the original bot exists in the Mini App (feature map, section 4).
- All package descriptions are identical to the original EN/AM text.
- Language switching works everywhere and is remembered.
- The service is blocked during working hours (section 3.1), in the UI and on the server.
- A booking in each of the 3 services reaches all 3 admins (photo + details + PDF) and the customer (PDF).
- The PDF is professionally formatted and renders Amharic correctly.
- The app works on iOS and Android Telegram, light and dark theme, small screens (360 px wide), slow networks.
- All automated tests pass and the manual QA checklist (section 13.4) is signed off on a real phone.

3. Business rules (non-negotiable)
3.1 Working hours
Offline window: 08:00–20:00 Africa/Addis_Ababa on the standard clock = 2:00 AM–2:00 PM Ethiopian time. The team answers phones then; customers must call.
Outside that window (20:00–08:00 standard = 2:00 PM–2:00 AM Ethiopian) customers book directly.
Ethiopian clock rule: Ethiopian hour = ((standard hour − 6) mod 12), with 0 shown as 12. So 08:00 -> 2 (morning), 20:00 -> 2 (evening).
While offline: the whole app shows the bilingual "Bot is Currently Offline" screen with a tap-to-call button, a "Check Again" button and access to the Contact page. POST /api/bookings returns 403 {error:"offline"}. The bot's /start shows the same message.
Hours are configurable (OFFLINE_START_HOUR, OFFLINE_END_HOUR) but default to 8 and 20. Existing app/hours.py already implements this; keep it.

3.2 Payment
Customer pays 50% of the package price by bank deposit, then uploads a screenshot. Booking is not complete until the deposit is received.
Bank details, default values (override by env): Commercial Bank of Ethiopia (CBE), account name Sara Mohammed, account number from the original code (BANK_ACCOUNT_NUMBER). Keep them in config.py only, never hard-code elsewhere.
PDF terms: provisional until payment is confirmed; deposit within 24 hours; balance due on the day of service; cancellations under 48 hours may incur charges.

3.3 Prices
Server is the source of truth for prices; the client never sends a price.
Known issue: the original bot's limo prices (25,000 / 30,000 / 35,000 ETB) disagree with the catalog PDF (35,000 / 45,000 / 50,000 ETB). The owner will decide later. Build tools/set_price.py (WP2) so that one command updates both the numeric price and the price inside the verbatim EN/AM title lines. Do not change any price before the owner confirms.

3.4 Booking references
<PREFIX>-<YYYYMMDDHHMMSS> in Africa/Addis_Ababa time, prefixes DEC, LIMO, PHOTO.

3.5 Where the Mini App intentionally differs from the bot
Original | Mini App
8–9 chat questions one by one | 2 form steps + payment step, with inline validation
Free-text date ("21/08/2018, Morning 4:00 AM") | Ethiopian-calendar pickers (day, month, year, hour, period) that produce the same text format, and show the Gregorian equivalent
Typed Telegram username | Pre-filled from Telegram; optional; falls back to the real Telegram account
Payment screenshot sent as a chat photo | Uploaded in the app, compressed in the browser
Duplicate start, show_menu, after_hours_handler functions | Removed; one implementation each

5. Content pipeline (exactness guarantee)
tools/extract_content.py parses tools/original_main.py with ast.literal_eval and writes:
app/data/packages.json: for each of 10 packages id, service, price, title{en,am}, text{en,am} (verbatim, Telegram-Markdown), links[]. IDs: decor_basic, decor_deluxe, decor_premium, limo_grand, limo_special, limo_royal, photo_digital, photo_standard, photo_premium, videography (service photo).
app/data/ui_strings.json: welcome, btns (5), contact_text, agree_btn, back, change_lang, q_back, discover_after_decor/limo/photo, discover_complete (has {booked_services}), book_now for en and am.
It asserts a byte-for-byte round trip. Never edit the extracted text by hand. Re-run the script if the original changes.
The strings that live inside handlers (not in CONTENT) must also be copied verbatim by script or by careful copy from tools/original_main.py, into app/data/strings.json (WP1):
offline (bilingual offline notice, with {phone}), payment_notice (with {account_name}, {account_number}), per-service upload_prompt, upload_error, client_caption ("✅ Your booking is awaiting confirmation. / ማረጋገጫ በመጠበቅ ላይ።"), catalog_caption, catalog_missing, welcome_choose.
discover_single (three one-service-left messages, EN+AM), discover_generic, discover_buttons (Book Decor / Limousine / Photography / Contact Us, EN+AM), back_to_packages.
app/data/forms.json defines each service form (section 7.2). Question texts and Amharic labels are copied from the d_step*, l_step*, ph_step* handlers.

Markdown rendering
The original strings use Telegram Markdown plus quirks. webapp/js/md.js must render, without leftover markup: *bold*, _italic_, [text](url), bare URLs, lines starting with • or - as bullets, ____/━━━ rules, and the legacy markers ••Title••, •Label:•, •Item found in contact and discover texts. Escape HTML first. Test against all 10 package texts and the UI strings in both languages: no *, [, or stray • may remain.

7. Data contracts
7.1 Environment variables
Names match the original bot. BOT_TOKEN, ADMIN_ID_1/2/3, PUBLIC_BASE_URL (falls back to RENDER_EXTERNAL_URL, then WEBHOOK_BASE_URL), WEBHOOK_PATH (default agos-bot), WEBHOOK_SECRET_TOKEN (letters, digits, _, - only), PORT (10000), LOGO_PATH, SERVICES_PDF_PATH. New: BANK_NAME, BANK_ACCOUNT_NAME, BANK_ACCOUNT_NUMBER, OFFLINE_START_HOUR, OFFLINE_END_HOUR, DEV_MODE (local only, see 13.1). Never log the token.

7.2 forms.json
Per service (decor, limo, photo): prefix, icon, title{en,am}, pdf_title, admin_title (HTML), file_prefix, client_file, fields[], steps[][] (field ids per step), admin_rows, pdf_rows (sections of [label, fieldId]). Field: id, type (text|phone|username|textarea|choice|package|datetime), label{en,am}, optional hint, optional, options[{v,en,am}].

Service | Step 1 | Step 2
decor | name, gender (Male/Female/Not Sure), phone, username, alt phone | address, house type (Villa/Apartment/Condominium/G+1/G+2), package, date
limo | name, phone, username | date, pickup & destination, package
photo | name, phone, username | event date, event address, package

Step 3 is always payment. Labels, hints and examples (e.g. "(e.g., 21/08/2018, Morning 4:00 AM)") are copied from the original prompts.

7.3 HTTP API
Every /api/* request carries header X-Telegram-Init-Data. Invalid or older than 24 h -> 401.

Route | Purpose
GET /health | {ok:true}
GET /api/config | {packages, forms, strings, ui, bank{name,account_name,account_number,deposit_percent}, contact{phones,website}, hours, user{id,first_name,username}}
POST /api/bookings | multipart: payload (JSON string) + screenshot (image)
POST /api/catalog | sends the catalog PDF to the user's chat, {sent:bool}
POST /{WEBHOOK_PATH} | Telegram updates; checks X-Telegram-Bot-Api-Secret-Token
GET /app/* | static Mini App, Cache-Control: no-cache

payload: service, package, name, phone, username, alt_phone, address, gender, house, lang, date{day,month,year,hour(1-12),period(morning|afternoon|evening|night)}.

Responses for POST /api/bookings:
Code | Body
200 | {ref, service, price, deposit, pdf_sent}; a repeat of the same submission within 10 min returns {ref, duplicate:true, pdf_sent:true} without re-notifying admins
403 | {error:"offline", hours}
422 | {error:"validation", errors:{field:code}} (codes invalid, past)
429 | {error:"rate_limited"} (6 bookings/hour/user)
502 | {error:"delivery_failed"} only when zero admins were reached

7.4 Validation (same rules client and server; server is authoritative)
Name 2–80 chars, address 3–400, whitespace collapsed.
Phone: Ethiopian mobile (+251|251|0)?[79]\d{8} normalised to +2517…/+2519…; otherwise any +?\d{8,15}.
Telegram username optional; @ optional on input; [A-Za-z0-9_]{3,32}; stored with @.
Choice fields must be one of the defined option values; package must exist and belong to the chosen service.
Date must be a valid Ethiopian date (months 1–12 have 30 days, Pagume has 5 or 6) and not in the past (compare in Addis Ababa time).
Screenshot <= 10 MB, must decode as an image; server converts to JPEG, max 2000 px, quality 85.
Date text format (matches the original): DD/MM/YYYY, Morning 4:00 (Amharic variant ጥዋት 4፡00). Also keep the Gregorian equivalent.

7.5 Ethiopian calendar (verified algorithm, implement in Python and JS)
To Gregorian: jdn = 1723856 + 365*y + floor(y/4) + 30*m + d − 31; Python date = date.fromordinal(jdn − 1721425); JS date = (jdn − 2440588) days after 1970-01-01 UTC.
From Gregorian: compute jdn; r = (jdn − 1723856) mod 1461; n = r mod 365 + 365*floor(r/1460); year = 4*floor((jdn − 1723856)/1461) + floor(r/365) − floor(r/1460); month = floor(n/30)+1; day = n mod 30 + 1.
Leap year: y mod 4 == 3 (Pagume has 6 days).
Known checks: 2019-01-01 (Meskerem 1) = 11 Sep 2026; 2018-08-21 = 29 Apr 2026; 2015-13-06 = 11 Sep 2023 (previous day). Test a 5,000-date round trip in both languages.

8. Backend specification
catalog.py: lru_cache JSON loaders; deposit(price) = round(price*0.5); money(n) -> "25,000 ETB"; public_config().
bookings.py: pydantic model, parse_and_validate(json) -> clean | BookingError, build_record(clean, user) (ref, price, deposit, balance, display values, user), in-memory rate_limited, dedupe_key (sha256 of user+payload+image), seen_recently, remember.
main.py: lifespan builds the PTB Application with updater(None), initializes, starts, sets webhook (with secret, drop_pending_updates) and the chat menu button (MenuButtonWebApp). current_user dependency validates initData (or X-Dev-User only if DEV_MODE=1). Order inside POST /api/bookings: hours gate -> rate limit -> read file (limit 10 MB+1) -> validate image -> validate payload -> dedupe -> build record -> build PDF -> deliver -> remember. Return the 422 shape above.
bot.py: /start and /menu -> offline message or welcome with an inline web_app button (never a reply-keyboard web_app button: those do not provide initData). Handlers for check_again and cfm|rej:<ref>:<userId> (admins only). Any private text message replies like /start. Webhook is the only update path.
Failure rules: one admin failing never blocks others; client PDF failure sets pdf_sent:false and the UI tells the customer to press Start in the bot; only 0 admins reached is an error.

9. PDF specification (app/pdf.py)
reportlab platypus, A4, one page for normal bookings.
Fonts: register Noto, Noto-B, Eth, Eth-B from app/fonts (Noto Sans and Noto Sans Ethiopic). Wrap Ethiopic runs (\u1200–\u139F, \u2D80–\u2DDF, \uAB00–\uAB2F) in <font name="Eth">; strip glyphs outside the fonts (emoji, ≈); escape XML.
Header on every page: logo (webp -> PNG via Pillow, left), "AGOS POSTPARTUM CARE" and website (right), gold rule. Footer: official-document line, both phone numbers, website and email, reference and page number.
Body: service title; status pill "PENDING - Awaiting 50% Deposit Confirmation"; meta block (reference, date, time Addis Ababa); dark section bars with gold underline; zebra tables; sections from pdf_rows, hiding empty optional rows; 3. PAYMENT INFORMATION (status, bank, account name/number, package price, remaining balance, bold required deposit); 4. TERMS AND CONDITIONS (4 bullets from 3.2). Keep sections together; no orphan headings.
Palette: gold #B8892E, ink #1C1C1C, cream #FBF5EC, line #E6D9C3.
Test: generate all 3 services with Amharic names/addresses and long text (400 chars); rasterise with pdftoppm and check visually; text must be extractable.

10. Admin delivery (app/notify.py)
For each admin, in parallel: send_photo (screenshot) with an HTML caption, then send_document (PDF named <Service>_<Name>.pdf). Customer gets the PDF with the original caption.
Caption mirrors the original summary: title, reference, date, time, section 1 client information, section 2 service details, section 3 payment information (bank, account, price, deposit, status PENDING), the verification reminder, client phone and Telegram. Use parse_mode=HTML with html.escape on every user value.
Telegram caption limit is 1024 characters. If longer, the photo gets a short caption and the full text is a separate message that carries the buttons.
Inline buttons: ✅ Confirm payment (cfm:<ref>:<userId>), ❌ Reject (rej:…), 💬 Message client (URL https://t.me/<username>, else tg://user?id=<id>; if Telegram rejects the tg:// link as private, resend without it). callback_data stays under 64 bytes.
On decision: only admins in ADMIN_IDS may act; notify the customer (bilingual, confirmed or could-not-verify with phone); edit every admin copy to append "CONFIRMED/REJECTED by <name>" and remove the buttons; a second decision answers "Already confirmed/rejected". Keep an in-memory registry of message ids (best effort; decisions must still work after a restart, they just update only the pressed message).

11. Frontend specification
Vanilla HTML/CSS/ES modules, no build step. Include https://telegram.org/js/telegram-web-app.js.

11.1 Routes (hash router)
#/ home · #/svc/<service> packages · #/pkg/<id> detail · #/book/<service>/<pkg>/<step 1–3> form · #/done/<ref> success · #/contact. When offline every route except contact renders the closed screen.

11.2 Behaviour
Load /api/config once per minute (always on home and closed screens). Show skeletons while loading; on 401 show "open from Telegram"; on network failure show retry.
Telegram integration: ready(), expand(), disableVerticalSwipes(), theme sync (colorScheme, setHeaderColor, setBackgroundColor, themeChanged event), native BackButton (previous step or history back), HapticFeedback on taps and success/error, enableClosingConfirmation() while a form has unsaved input, openLink / openTelegramLink for external links.
Form: stepper with 3 steps; fields from forms.json; chips for short choices; radio cards for packages (title + price); date pickers (day list adapts to month length, years = current Ethiopian year to +3) with the Gregorian equivalent shown; Ethiopian phone auto-format to +251… on blur; pre-fill name and username from Telegram; draft per service saved in localStorage and cleared after success; inline error messages in the current language; focus and scroll to the first error; Next validates the current step only; Submit validates everything and jumps to the step with the first error.
Payment step: package price, 50% deposit (large), balance; bank card with copy buttons; original bilingual notice (rendered by md.js); screenshot picker with preview, compressed in-browser (canvas, max 1600 px, JPEG 0.82).
Submit: disable button, show "Sending…", map 422/403/429/502 to messages, never lose form data on error. After success remember booked services in localStorage.
Done screen: check animation, reference, original client caption, PDF-sent note (or fallback note), cross-sell with the original logic: 2 services left -> discover_after_<booked>; 1 left -> matching single message; 0 left -> discover_complete with the booked list and Contact button; Menu button always.
Contact: hours, tap-to-call numbers, Instagram, TikTok, Telegram, website, map link, all from the original contact text.
Visual design: cream and gold palette from the catalog and logo, dark mode, large 48 px touch targets, bottom sheet-style sticky action bar with safe-area padding, card layout, 20 px radii, skeleton loaders, reduced-motion support. Fonts: system stack plus Noto Sans Ethiopic fallback. Amharic text must never be clipped or truncated.
Accessibility: labels on all inputs, aria-live for the content region, sufficient contrast in both themes, keyboard operable.

12. Security and privacy
Validate initData on every API call; reject anything older than 24 h; use constant-time comparison. Never trust client user ids or prices.
Verify the webhook secret header. Escape all user text in Telegram HTML and in the DOM (textContent or an escape helper; the Markdown renderer escapes first).
Limit upload size, decode the image server-side, re-encode as JPEG (strips metadata).
Rate limit and dedupe (section 7.3). In-memory is acceptable for one instance; document this.
Do not log tokens, phone numbers or screenshots. No bookings are stored on disk in v1 (admins receive them in Telegram).
The original bot printed its token to the logs: rotate the token in BotFather (/revoke) before going live. Move bank details to env vars.

13. Testing plan

DEV_MODE=1 accepts an X-Dev-User JSON header and writes PDFs, images and admin text to /tmp/agos_dev_out instead of calling Telegram; the frontend uses ?dev=1. Must be impossible to enable by accident in production (log a loud warning).

13.2 Unit and API (pytest)
Existing 9 tests plus: auth (valid, tampered, expired); hours boundaries (7, 8, 13, 19, 20, 23, 0) and Ethiopian clock mapping; calendar round trip; each service books successfully; duplicate submit not re-sent; every validation error; bad image; offline blocks server-side; phone normalisation; notify with a fake bot (all admins + customer, one admin failing, long caption fallback, confirm notifies customer once, second decision refused).

13.3 Browser end-to-end (Playwright, 390×844, dev mode)
For EN and AM: home -> each service -> package -> book -> fill -> upload -> success; back button behaviour; validation errors; offline screen with a mocked clock; language switch mid-form keeps data; refresh restores draft; copy-account button; cross-sell variants; contact links; no console errors; no horizontal scroll at 360 px.

13.4 Manual QA on a real phone (Android and iPhone)
Open from inline button and from the menu button; light and dark theme; submit a booking in all 3 services outside working hours; confirm 3 admins receive photo, details, PDF and buttons; Confirm and Reject notify the customer; open the PDF on the phone (Amharic correct); try during working hours (blocked); try as a user who blocked the bot (UI shows the PDF fallback).

15. Risks and known limits
- Untested against real Telegram until WP8: updater(None) webhook mode in PTB 22.8, MenuButtonWebApp, tg://user?id= links in admin buttons.
- initData expires after 24 h: a customer who leaves the app open for days must reopen it (the UI shows a clear message).
- A customer who never pressed Start or blocked the bot cannot receive the PDF; booking still reaches admins.
- In-memory state (rate limit, dedupe, decision registry) resets on restart.
- Cross-sell state is per device (localStorage), unlike the original per-chat session.
- Catalog and package photos in the PDF are large (the catalog is ~3.4 MB); compress before shipping inside the app.

16. Open decisions for the owner
- Correct limo prices (bot vs catalog) and any other price changes.
- Keep "discover more" cross-sell per device, or per Telegram user once a database exists?
- Add booking history and status tracking (WP9)?
